Back tothe Board

One player per team sits with their back to the board. Their team shouts examples until they name the category.

How to run it

  1. One player per team turns away from the board.
  2. Teams shout examples. Never say the category!
  3. First player to say it wins: tap that podium.
  4. Swap players every few rounds.
