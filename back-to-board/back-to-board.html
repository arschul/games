Back to the Board

One player from each team sits with their back to the board. Their team shouts examples until the player names the category.

How to run it

  1. Pick one player per team. They turn their chairs away from the screen.
  2. A category appears. Both teams shout example words at their own player — never the category itself.
  3. The first player to say the category out loud wins the round. Tap that team's button.
  4. Swap players every few rounds so everyone gets a turn facing away.
