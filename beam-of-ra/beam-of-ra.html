Beam of Ra

A laser-and-mirror strategy game for two players or two teams. Hit the other Pharaoh to win.
How to play
  1. On your turn, do one thing: move a piece one square (any direction) or rotate it 90°.
  2. Then fire your laser. It bounces off mirrors and travels in straight lines.
  3. A piece hit on a plain side is destroyed — even your own.
  4. A Scarab may swap places with a next-door Pyramid or Anubis.
  5. Tinted squares are for one color only.
  6. Hit the enemy Pharaoh to win.
