Beam of Ra

A laser-and-mirror strategy game for two players or two teams. Bounce your beam into the other Pharaoh to win.
How to play
  1. On your turn, do one thing: move a piece one square (any direction) or turn it 90°. The Pharaoh cannot turn.
  2. Then fire your laser. It bounces off mirrors and travels in straight lines.
  3. A piece hit on a plain side is destroyed, even your own.
  4. A Scarab can swap places with a Pyramid or Anubis next to it.
  5. Tinted squares are for one color only.
  6. Hit the enemy Pharaoh to win.
How pieces react
Q/E turn, Enter confirm, Space fire, Esc cancel, Shift+U undo, M sound, Shift+W weather, Shift+N music, F fullscreen
