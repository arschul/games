How to play

  1. On your turn, place one tile from your supply in your park. It must touch a tile you already placed and may not cover a Pit.
  2. Every icon you cover gives you something: wheelbarrow → a Green Area, cement truck → an Animal House, excavator → an Enclosure, construction crew → a new Park Area.
  3. Fill a whole Park Area (everything except the Pit) and you get the best Bear Statue left.
  4. Can't place anything? Pass and take a free Green Area.
  5. When a team fills all four Park Areas, everyone else gets one last turn. Points: Animal Houses + Enclosures + Bear Statues.

Rules summary

  1. Place one tile from your supply in your park. It must fit inside your Park Areas, may not overlap, may not cover a Pit, and must touch a tile you placed before (your very first tile can go anywhere). Rotate and flip freely.
  2. Icons you cover: wheelbarrow → any Green Area · cement truck → top Animal House of any species (or a Green Area) · excavator → any Enclosure (or an Animal House or Green Area) · construction crew → a new Park Area, attached right away next to your park, never below your Entrance. Maximum four Park Areas.
  3. Bear Statues: when a Park Area is completely filled (except its Pit), put the highest Bear Statue left on the Pit.
  4. Pass only if nothing in your supply fits: take a free Green Area instead.
  5. End: when a team fills all four Park Areas, every other team gets one more turn. Score Animal Houses + Enclosures + Bear Statues (+ Achievements). Tie: most points still in your supply.
  6. Achievements: the first team to meet a goal takes its top tile; one tile of each kind per team.
