How to play

  1. Listen to the word. Press Hear word or In a sentence as often as you need. Then type it.
  2. With 2, 4, 8 or 16 players, you go straight into the hive (the bracket). With any other number, everyone first spells two words in a seeding round, and the best spellers go on.
  3. In a duel, both players get a different word with the same number of letters. A correct spelling beats a wrong one.
  4. Both correct? Fewer edits wins (every Backspace counts). Then the player who is 8 or more seconds faster. Still tied? A tiebreak with harder words.
  5. Both wrong? The closer spelling wins. British spellings like colour also count.
  6. Words get harder each round. Win every duel to take the crown!
Keys: Enter = check / next · B = bracket · S = stats · H = help · M = sound · T = theme · Shift+N = music
