    Word Bluff
    Add one letter at a time. Keep a real word possible. Call the bluff.
    Players 4
    Hearts
    Timer
    Sound
    How to play
    1. Take turns. Add one letter to the end of the word.
    2. Every new letter must keep a real English word possible. C-A-T could become CATCH.
    3. Think the last player is bluffing? Press Challenge. They must say and spell their word.
      Real word: the challenger loses a heart. No real word: the bluffer loses a heart.
    4. If your letter finishes a real word (4+ letters), you lose a heart. The teacher presses Full word.
    5. Give up or run out of time: you lose a heart.
    Lose all your hearts and you are out. The last player in the game wins.
