CEFR Quest
A grammar board game for A1–B2 classes. Race around the school and win all four level crests.

Teams

Tap a badge to change the animal. Switch teams in or out (2 to 6 play).

Levels in play

Rounds

Win by holding all 4 crests. If nobody does, the game ends after the last round: most crests, then most correct answers, then most points.

How to play

  1. Roll and move. On your turn, roll the die and hop that many squares clockwise.
  2. Answer the card. Question squares give points when you get them right. A wrong answer sends you back the number you rolled.
  3. Hubs. Land on a corner with 25 points, answer correctly, then pay 25 points to claim that level's crest.
  4. Magnetic pull. Stop 1 or 2 squares short of a crest you still need and it may pull you in.
  5. Duels. Challenge a rival. Win and you steal 25% of their points. Lose and they take 25% of yours.
  6. Double or Nothing. Risk some points on a question. Right: you gain them. Wrong: you lose them.
  7. Shop. Banana peels, a heavy backpack for a rival, or skip the test and take a crest for 50 points.
  8. The teacher is the referee. Reveal the answer, then decide: Correct or Wrong. Nothing moves on until you click.
