Castle Roads

A tile-laying game in the style of Carcassonne · 2–5 players

Players

2

Who starts

How to play

  1. Draw a tile and add it to the map. Every edge must match: city to city, road to road, field to field.
  2. You may put one follower on the tile you just placed — but not on a road, city or field someone already has.
  3. Finished road: 1 point per tile. Finished city: 2 per tile and 2 per shield. Monastery with 8 neighbors: 9.
  4. Followers come home when their feature scores. Farmers stay until the end.
  5. At the end, unfinished things score less, and farmers score 3 for each finished city next to their field.
