How to play

  1. The board shows two categories. On your turn, say one item for each category.
  2. The teacher decides. Correct gives you 3 more seconds and the next team plays. Wrong takes 5 seconds and gives you a strike.
  3. No repeats: an answer that someone already said this round is wrong.
  4. Each team has one clock for the whole round. It only runs on your turn.
  5. You are out when your clock reaches 0:00 or you get too many strikes.
  6. The last team left wins the round and gets a star. Get enough stars to win the game.
  7. Once per round you can use each ticket: Switch (new categories), Skip (pass your turn) or Extra (until your next turn, the other teams must answer three categories).
