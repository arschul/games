How to play
Each round, the narrator gives you a starting place and a set of walking directions through the city. Follow them in your head across the map. The token starts on the green START square; the directions lead to one of the other places.

Easy & Medium use compass directions and street names — "Go two blocks north on C Avenue. Turn... " — no need to track which way you're facing.
Hard uses your point of view — "You are facing north. Go straight two blocks. Turn left, then go three blocks." — so you must keep track of the way you're facing.

When the directions finish, teams take turns (the order rotates each round). On your turn, tap the place on the map where you think you end up. Tap Reveal Answer once everyone has guessed: the correct place scores +1. After the set number of rounds, the highest score wins.

Streets run across (1st–8th Street); Avenues run down (A–H Avenue). Every place has an emoji — check the Places in this city legend if you forget which is which. Directions always keep you on the map.
