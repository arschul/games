How to play
Each round the narrator gives you a starting corner and walking directions through the city. Follow them in your head. The last instruction is always "It's on your left" or "It's on your right" — that tells you which side of the street the place is on.

Easy & Medium use compass directions and street names — "Go two blocks north on C Avenue. Then go one block east on 2nd Street. It's on your left."
Hard uses your point of view — "You are facing north. Go straight two blocks. Turn right on 2nd Street and go one block. It's on your left." — so you must track which way you are facing.

When the directions finish, teams take turns (the order rotates each round). On your turn, tap the building you think the directions lead to. Tap Reveal Answer once everyone has guessed: the correct place scores +1. After the set number of rounds, the highest score wins.

Streets run across the city (1st–4th Street). Avenues run up and down (A–E Avenue). Every building is labelled on the map, and the Places in this city panel lists them all.
