How to play

The idea

  • Teams take turns adding letters to the board, then race to build words from the letters on it.
  • Longer words are worth much more: 3 letters = 3 points, 4 = 5, 5 = 8, 6 = 12, then +3 for each extra letter. Two-letter words (if allowed) score 1 point.
  • Each word can be claimed only once per game, by anyone.
  • If the teacher set a theme, every word must fit it.

On your turn

  • Click an empty square and type a letter. The small blocks next to the clock show how many letters you have left.
  • Backspace removes a letter you just placed. Double-click it to retype it. Arrow keys move to the next free square.
  • When the turn ends, your letters are inked in your team's color. They stay on the board for everyone.
  • Read squares aloud with the numbers: row on the left, then column on the top. "An S at 34" is row 30, column 4.
  • The starting team changes from game to game. It gets a head start: 1 extra token with powers on, or 30 extra seconds on the first turn with powers off.

Claiming a word (any team, any time)

  • Choose your panel first: the one with the gold outline gets your clicks. Press 1, 2 or 3 to switch.
  • Words must be a straight line of touching squares (across, down or diagonal), clicked in reading order. Backwards along the line is fine.
  • Click a letter already in your word to cut the word back to that point.
  • The teacher is the judge: real words only, no names or abbreviations. The ✕ on a word removes it.
  • Scroll the mouse wheel over a team's panel to zoom in on its words.

Powers (optional)

  • Each team starts with 1 token and gets another when it ends a turn without scoring. Maximum 3.
  • One power per turn, on your own turn only.
  • Extra letter (1): place one more letter this turn.
  • Double word (2): the next word your team claims scores double. One at a time.
  • Shield (1): lock any square. The other teams can't type there, use it in a word, or move, retype or swap it. It opens again when your next turn starts.
  • Move (2): move a locked letter to an empty square.
  • Retype (2): pick a locked letter and type a different one. It becomes your team's letter.
  • Swap (2): swap two locked letters.
  • Words already claimed keep their points. The clock stops while you choose a square. Esc cancels.

The clock

  • When time runs out, the turn ends and the letters are locked.
  • Fast finish: if the team whose turn it is claims a word with a letter it just placed, the clock drops to 20 seconds.
  • When the board is full, each team gets one final turn to claim words and use tokens.

Teacher controls

  • P pause · E end turn · U undo · Enter claim · 1/2/3 pick panel · ? rules · M sound · Shift+N music · Esc close.
