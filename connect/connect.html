How to Play
The idea
  • Teams take turns adding letters to the board, then race to build words out of the letters already on it.
  • Longer words are worth much more: 3 letters → 3 points · 4 → 5 · 5 → 8 · 6 → 12, then +3 for every extra letter.
  • Minimum 3 letters. Every word can only be claimed once per game, by anyone.
  • If the teacher has set a theme, it shows above the board and every word must fit it.
On your turn
  • Click an empty square and type a letter. The dots above the board show how many letters you have left.
  • Backspace removes a letter you just placed. Click a letter twice to retype it.
  • Letters lock in your team's colour when the turn ends. They stay on the board for everyone.
  • Read positions aloud using the numbers: the row down the side plus the column across the top — "an S at 34" is row 30, column 4. Hovering a square shows its number.
  • The starting team rotates from game to game, and whoever starts gets one extra letter.
Claiming a word (any team, any time)
  • Choose your panel first — the one outlined in gold receives your clicks. Press 1, 2 or 3 to switch.
  • Words must sit on a straight line of touching squares — across, down, or diagonal — and be clicked in reading order. Backwards along the line is fine.
  • Click a letter already in your word to cut the word back to that point.
  • The panel shows the word's coordinates (e.g. 13→15) so it can be read out and checked.
  • The teacher is the judge — real words only, no names or abbreviations — and can remove a claimed word with the ✕ on its tag.
The clock
  • Each turn has a timer. When it runs out the turn ends automatically and any letters placed are locked.
  • Fast Finish: if the team whose turn it is claims a word using a letter it just placed, the turn drops to 20 seconds. Turn it off in setup.
Teacher controls
  • ⏸ pauses the clock. "Show Board" keeps the board visible with the clock still frozen — nothing can be typed or claimed while paused.
  • ↶ Undo reverses the last claim, turn end, or word removal.
  • Keys: P pause · E end turn · U undo · 1/2/3 pick panel · Enter claim · ? rules · Esc close.
End the game?
Scores will be final and the board will lock. This can't be undone.
