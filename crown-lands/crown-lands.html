Game

Options

Build the richest kingdom

1Place

Put your domino in your kingdom. It must touch your castle or the same landscape.

2Choose

Put your king on a domino for next round. Top of the list plays first.

3Stay small

Your kingdom can only be 5 squares wide and 5 squares tall.

4Count crowns

Each landscape scores squares × crowns. No crowns, no points.
