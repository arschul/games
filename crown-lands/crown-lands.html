Variants

Build the richest kingdom

1Pick

Put your king on a domino in the next line. A low number moves you early next round; a high number is usually worth more.

2Connect

Each new domino must touch your castle or a square of the same landscape.

3Stay in the frame

Your kingdom can never be wider or taller than 5 squares. No legal spot? The domino is lost.

4Count crowns

Every connected landscape scores its squares × its crowns. No crowns, no points.
