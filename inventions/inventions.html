How to play

  1. Every team gets 5 cards. One card starts on the rail with its year showing.
  2. On your turn, choose a card from your hand. Read the clue together and discuss in English.
  3. Click a + on the rail: before, after or between the cards there.
  4. Right? The card stays on the rail. Wrong? It is thrown away and you get a new card.
  5. If two cards have the same year, either order is right.
  6. The first team with an empty hand wins, but the round always finishes first. If two teams finish in the same round, the faster team wins.
  7. When time runs out, the teacher decides: lose the turn or get more time.
