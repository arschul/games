How to play

Forge a word

Tap letters in any order (or type them). They don't need to touch. Then press Attack!

Each letter has points. Longer words hit harder: 4+ letters ×1.3, 6+ ×1.6, 8+ ×1.9, 10+ ×2.2.

The word must be in the word list. If it isn't, the teacher can press Accept anyway.

Power

Taking damage fills your power bar. At 15 you can use one ability. Each ability works once per game.

Time

When the time runs out, you lose your turn.

Abilities

    Solo

    Beat wave after wave of monsters. Your HP, board and power carry over. Each wave cleared gives +20 HP. Extra damage on a monster turns into power.
