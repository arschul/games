How to play

  1. 1Split. Every team gets the same number of tiles and flips them face up.
  2. 2Build. Make one connected crossword. Words read left to right or top to bottom. Move tiles any time.
  3. 3Peel. Used every tile? Say Peel! Every team takes one new tile from the Bunch.
  4. 4Dump. Stuck with a tile? Put it back in the Bunch and take three.
  5. 5Bananas! When the Bunch has fewer tiles than there are teams, the first team to use every tile wins.
  6. 6Rotten. A bad word at the end puts your team out and your tiles go back to the Bunch.

No names, abbreviations or hyphenated words. Keys: Space pause · P peel · U undo · N next team · M sound · Shift+N music · T theme
