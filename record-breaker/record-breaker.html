How to play

  1. Read the record. Teams take turns to guess the number. Every guess must be higher than the last one.
  2. Think the last guess is too high? Press No way! If you're right, you get 1 point, and the closest guess under the record gets 1 point too.
  3. If you're wrong, your team is out for this record. The last team standing gets 1 point.
  4. When the tower is full, it's a showdown: the closest guess under the record gets 1 point.
  5. Teacher: ✕ Strike (or Backspace) removes a silly guess. That team guesses again.
