Tangled Paths

A path-building game in the style of Tsuro, for 2 to 8 players

Players

2

Who starts

Match

How to play

  1. Each player starts a dragon on a mark at the edge and holds 3 tiles.
  2. Put one tile, turned any way, on the square your dragon faces.
  3. Every dragon touching the new tile follows its path to the end.
  4. Leave the board or crash into another dragon and you're out. You can't leave if any tile, turned any way, keeps you on.
  5. The last dragon on the board wins.
