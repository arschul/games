Tangled Paths

A path-building game in the style of Tsuro · 2–8 players

Players

2

Who starts

How to play

  1. Each player puts a stone on a mark at the edge of the board and holds 3 tiles.
  2. On your turn, put one tile, turned any way, on the square your stone faces.
  3. Every stone touching the new tile slides along its path to the end.
  4. Leave the board, or crash into another stone, and you are out.
  5. Don't send yourself off the board if any tile, turned any way, avoids it. Last stone standing wins.
