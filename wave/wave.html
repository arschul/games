Wave

Two teams. One clue. Can your team find the hidden target on the dial?

Play to

Cards up to level

Your own cards (optional — one per line, like: Boring film | Great film)

How to play

  1. Teams take turns. On your turn, one student is the psychic.
  2. Read the card. It shows two opposite ideas, one on each side of the dial.
  3. Tap Spin the target. The target hides behind the screen.
  4. Everyone else looks away. The psychic opens the screen, sees the target, and closes it.
  5. The psychic says one clue: a word or a short phrase that sits in the right place between the two ideas.
  6. The team talks in English and moves the red needle. Then they lock it in.
  7. The other team guesses: is the target to the left or to the right of the needle?
  8. Open the screen and score. A correct left or right call gives the other team 1 point.
