How to play

  1. Spin the wheel, then say a consonant. You win the points on the wheel for every time it is in the sentence.
  2. Right letter? Spin again, buy a vowel for 250, or solve. Wrong letter? The next team plays.
  3. The storm grows every time you spin or buy a vowel. More Lose a turn and Bankrupt spaces appear. Bankrupt takes all your round points!
  4. Solve the sentence to keep your round points. The other teams lose theirs.
  5. No consonants left? Each team gets one free vowel, then solves or passes.
Keys: letters guess, Space spin, 1 buy a vowel, 2 pass, 3 hint, Enter solve or next, 4 setup, 9 teacher, Shift+M sound, Shift+N music, Shift+T day/night, Shift+D debug
